Holes are written column,strip: columns are counted along the strips from the left and strips from the top, with the board component side up as in `layout.svg`.

## Board

## 5. Checks before power

- Under a magnifier, check every cut is clean and no solder bridges two strips. No ground strip runs beside SENSE, SENDER, A1 or A2, so a bridge can't join those to ground, but fix any bridge you find.
- With a meter on continuity, and the module not yet powered, check each net joins only its own holes:

  - **+3V3:** J1.1 (1,3), U1.7 (6,3)
  - **AIN0:** C1.1 (13,4), Rref.1 (16,4), U1.2 (11,4)
  - **AIN1:** C1.2 (13,5), C2.1 (15,5), R1.1 (21,5), U1.3 (11,5)
  - **AIN2:** C2.2 (15,6), R2.1 (16,6), U1.4 (11,6)
  - **GND:** J1.2 (1,4), J2.4 (28,8), U1.8 (6,4)
  - **SCL:** J1.3 (1,5), U1.9 (6,5)
  - **SDA:** J1.4 (1,6), U1.10 (6,6)
  - **SENDER:** J2.1 (28,5), R1.2 (25,5), Rref.2 (20,4)
  - **SENSE:** J2.2 (28,6), R2.2 (20,6)

- Then that no two of +3V3, GND, SCL, SDA, AIN0, AIN1, AIN2, SENDER and SENSE are joined.

## Box

- **Board:** 73.7 x 27.9 mm, 1.6 mm thick. Hold it by its two long edges in slots in the box sides (about 1 mm deep): nothing on the board comes within 1 mm of those edges.
- **J1 opening, left end:** 6.1 to 16.7 mm from the top long edge (10.6 mm wide), from the board surface up to 8.5 mm; leave room above it to turn the screws.
- **J2 opening, right end:** 11.2 to 21.8 mm from the top long edge (10.6 mm wide), from the board surface up to 8.5 mm; leave room above it to turn the screws.
- **Height:** the terminal blocks (8.5 mm) and the module on its header (about 7.5 mm) are the tallest parts. Allow 12 mm above the board and 4 mm below it.
- **The module** is held by its 12 soldered pins. A rib in the lid that bears lightly on it stops it rocking in a vibrating car.
