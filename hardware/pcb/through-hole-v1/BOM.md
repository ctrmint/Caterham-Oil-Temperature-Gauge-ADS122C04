# through-hole-v1: parts list

Every part for the through-hole-v1 interface board, its cables and its box. The same list is in `through-hole-v1-bom.csv`. Everything is through-hole and hand-soldered.

Generated by `generator/outputs.py` from `generator/design.py`; edit that, not this file, then run `build.sh`. Part numbers were checked against manufacturer datasheets and distributor pages on 2026-11-15. Order codes are Digi-Key UK (stock seen then); Farnell and RS carry most parts too.

## Connector pinouts

**J1, power (Micro-Fit 3.0, 1x3)**

| Pin | Net | Wire to |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | GND | Chassis ground near the tank bond; wire both pins for a lower-resistance return |
| 2 | GND | Chassis ground near the tank bond; wire both pins for a lower-resistance return |
| 3 | 12V_IN | Fused, ignition-switched 12 V |

**J2, sender harness (Micro-Fit 3.0, 1x3)**

| Pin | Net | Wire to |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | SENDER | Sender spade terminal (signal) |
| 2 | SENSE | Ground-sense wire to the tank bond stud |
| 3 | GND | Harness screen or drain, at this end only |
