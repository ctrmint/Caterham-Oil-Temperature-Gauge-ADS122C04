## Box

- **Board:** 86.4 x 30.5 mm, 1.6 mm thick. Hold it by its two long edges in slots in the box sides (about 1 mm deep): nothing on the board comes within 1 mm of those edges.
- **J3 opening, left end:** 6.1 to 21.8 mm from the top long edge (15.7 mm wide), from the board surface up to 8.5 mm; leave room above it to turn the screws.
- **J2 opening, right end:** 11.2 to 21.8 mm from the top long edge (10.6 mm wide), from the board surface up to 8.5 mm; leave room above it to turn the screws.
- **Height:** the terminal blocks are the tallest parts (8.5 mm). Allow 12 mm above the board and 4 mm below it for the solder side and trimmed leads.
- **The module** is held by its 12 soldered pins. A rib in the lid that bears lightly on its far end stops it rocking in a vibrating car.
