# surface-mount-v1: parts list

Every part for the surface-mount-v1 interface board and its harness. The same list is in `surface-mount-v1-bom.csv`, and the board-house subset in `fab/surface-mount-v1-bom-jlcpcb.csv`.

Generated by `generator/outputs.py` from `generator/design.py`; edit that, not this file, then run `build.sh`. Part numbers were checked against manufacturer datasheets and distributor pages on 2026-11-15. LCSC numbers are for the board house's assembly service; `hand` parts are fitted by you (see README.md).
